About

Hello & Welcome

I am an independent wealth manager specialising in investments and life insurance. My focus is on building trust and relationship with my clients in order to implement a holistic financial plan for them to cover all their needs and get them closer to reaching their goals and objectives. Wealth is not only one's finances but how your finances are setup to allow you time with those you love. I am also fascinated by mountaineering, especially the 8,000-metre peaks, and there are more similarities between climbing and wealth planning than most people expect — which is where the mountains on this site come from. Personal finances can feel like standing at the foot of one, but the best way up is to determine your route and start climbing.
